  Built a computer vision pipeline to process the blueprint archive at

  identify candidate interchangeable parts across the full set of
  drawings.

  Processed roughly 20,000 blueprints to surface interchangeable parts,
  cutting a real supply bottleneck instead of leaving it to manual
  engineering lookup.
